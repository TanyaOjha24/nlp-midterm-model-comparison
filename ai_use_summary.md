# AI Use Summary

## How I Used AI During This Project

During this project, I used ChatGPT mainly as a learning and support tool to clarify concepts, understand model architectures, and work through implementation challenges.

Since the assignment involved building three different language models, I used ChatGPT to ask questions whenever I was unfamiliar with a concept or needed a better explanation of how a particular component worked.

For example:
- **Bigram Model:** I explored how character transition probabilities work and how a probability matrix can be used to predict the next character.
- **LSTM:** I asked questions about recurrent connections, hidden states, memory cells, and gating mechanisms to better understand how an LSTM processes sequential data.
- **Transformer:** I used ChatGPT to clarify concepts such as self-attention, queries, keys, values, positional embeddings, multi-head attention, and causal masking.
- **Implementation Support:** While working in Google Colab, I used AI assistance to understand certain PyTorch components, clarify syntax, troubleshoot errors, and work through implementation issues.

## My Contributions and Learning

I worked through the notebook in Google Colab, ran the experiments, trained all three models on the Tiny Shakespeare dataset, and collected their results.

I independently examined the training and validation loss curves, compared the models' convergence patterns, and interpreted their performance across epochs. I also evaluated the generated text samples and compared the models based on their losses, parameter counts, execution times, and output quality.

Through this project, I developed a better understanding of how the three approaches differ in modeling sequential data, why an LSTM can retain information from previous characters, and how Transformer attention allows a model to capture relationships across a context window.

I also organized the experimental results, documented the observations, wrote the project reflection, and prepared the GitHub repository.

## Responsible Use of AI

I treated AI-generated suggestions as guidance rather than automatically accepting them. I reviewed explanations, tried implementations in my notebook, and checked the resulting outputs.

ChatGPT was useful when I needed clarification or got stuck, but I remained involved in the implementation, experimentation, evaluation, and interpretation of the results. My intention was to use AI to support my understanding of the models and complete the project while learning the underlying concepts.
